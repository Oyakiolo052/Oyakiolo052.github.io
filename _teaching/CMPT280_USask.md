  <section class="course280-section" id="overview">
    <h2>Course Overview</h2>

    <p>
      CMPT 280 introduces the design, implementation, and analysis
      of data structures and algorithms using Java and
      object-oriented programming principles.
    </p>

    <p>
      The course covers fundamental and advanced data structures,
      including lists, trees, dictionaries, heaps, and graphs,
      alongside searching, sorting, and algorithm analysis.
    </p>

    <p>
      A central emphasis is placed on understanding the strengths
      and limitations of different data structures, evaluating
      their computational efficiency, and selecting appropriate
      structures and algorithms to solve practical problems.
    </p>

  <!-- Course Topics -->
  <section class="course280-section" id="topics">
    <h2>Course Topics</h2>

    <p>
      The course covers the following interconnected areas
      of data structures, algorithms, and software development.
    </p>

    <div class="course280-topic">
      <h3>
        <i class="fa-solid fa-list"></i>
        Linear Data Structures and ADTs
      </h3>
      <p>
        Array-based lists, singly and doubly linked lists,
        cursors, iterators, stacks, queues, abstract data types,
        and formal specifications.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="course280-topic">
      <h3>
        <i class="fa-solid fa-code-branch"></i>
        Trees and Dictionaries
      </h3>
      <p>
        General and binary trees, tree traversals, heaps,
        ordered binary trees, AVL trees, hash tables,
        2-3 trees, B-trees, B+ trees, trie trees, and k-D trees.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="course280-topic">
      <h3>
        <i class="fa-solid fa-diagram-project"></i>
        Graph Algorithms
      </h3>
      <p>
        Directed and undirected graphs, graph representations,
        breadth-first search, depth-first search,
        and shortest-path algorithms including Dijkstra's algorithm.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="course280-topic">
      <h3>
        <i class="fa-solid fa-arrow-down-short-wide"></i>
        Sorting and Algorithm Analysis
      </h3>
      <p>
        Merge sort, quicksort, heapsort, bucket sort,
        radix sort, statement counting, active operations,
        Big-O, and Big-Theta notation.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="course280-topic">
      <h3>
        <i class="fa-solid fa-flask"></i>
        Software Testing
      </h3>
      <p>
        Regression testing, black-box testing, white-box testing,
        and management of expected and unexpected exceptions.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>
