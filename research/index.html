RESEARCH

Research Interests and Collaborations

My research lies at the intersection of Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence, with a focus on empirical software engineering, human-centered software development, and trustworthy AI-assisted software engineering.

I investigate how software development practices can become more effective, understandable, and trustworthy, particularly as AI coding agents increasingly participate in software development.

Current Research Projects

Research Direction

Engineering Trustworthy AI-Generated Software

Investigating how AI-generated software can be systematically evaluated for correctness, maintainability, security, and long-term software quality.

Research Direction

Human–AI Collaboration in Code Review

Understanding how developers evaluate, verify, and make acceptance decisions about AI-authored pull requests, including trust, verification effort, and review practices.
